Black Hole - a real-time spinning black hole you can fly into

To start: double-click black_hole.exe. Keep the shaders folder next to it.

You need a GPU with OpenGL 4.3 or newer and an up-to-date graphics driver.
Windows may warn about an unknown publisher, because the program isn't signed.
If so, click "More info", then "Run anyway".


CONTROLS

  F                switch between orbit camera and free flight
  K / L            spin down / up (the current spin is in the window title)
  T                accretion disk on / off
  G / P / B        spacetime grid / Doppler / bloom
  Space            pause the disk
  + / -            render resolution (press - if the frame rate is low)
  [ / ]            exposure
  1 / 2 / 3        camera presets
  R                reload the shaders
  H                print the controls
  Esc              leave free flight, or quit

  Orbit camera
  Left-drag        orbit
  Wheel            zoom
  O                auto-orbit

  Free flight
  W A S D          forward / left / back / right
  Q / E            down / up
  Shift            faster
  Mouse            look around
  Wheel            field of view


THINGS TO TRY

Press L until the spin reaches 0.998 and compare it with K down to 0.
Press F, hold W and fly into the hole. Keep going below the horizon, then
turn round with the mouse to see the stars behind you.
